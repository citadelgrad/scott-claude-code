# Skill Authorship and Provenance

[Scott Nixon (@citadelgrad)](https://github.com/citadelgrad) is the author or adaptation author of this repository's portable skills and the project-original plugin skills listed below. “Adaptation author” means Scott substantially reshaped an adopted idea or earlier skill for this project; it does not erase the original creator. Source links in individual skills remain authoritative.

Git history was used to verify repository authorship, but commit authorship alone was not treated as proof of original creation. Known copied and vendored skills are separated into their own section and retain their original creators' credit in [`plugins/review-panel/CREDITS.md`](plugins/review-panel/CREDITS.md).
